Network matching is non-negotiable: Wrong-network crypto sends are not recoverable in most cases. The deposit confirmation workflow on LongFu88 specifies the required network — do not proceed if your wallet’s network selection does not exactly match.

Seven Payment Habits That Prevent Problems

  • Always match withdrawal method to deposit method

    Anti-fraud workflows process symmetrical fund flows (same method in and out) faster and with fewer holds than mixed method combinations. If you deposited via Skrill, withdraw via Skrill first.

  • Submit KYC documents proactively

    Upload identity documents as soon as your account is registered. Pre-approved KYC eliminates the document review step from your first withdrawal — the single most common cause of extended wait times.

  • Label crypto wallet addresses and verify networks

    Wrong-network crypto sends are irreversible. Always triple-check the network label (TRC20 vs ERC20 vs Bitcoin mainnet) in both your wallet and LongFu88’s deposit interface before confirming any transaction. Send a test amount first to a new address.

  • Factor in FX when using cards or cross-currency wallets

    Your card issuer applies their own FX rate on cross-currency transactions. Calculate your true cost including the FX spread before comparing crypto vs card options for large deposits.

  • Keep e-wallet balances modest

    Only park your session entertainment budget in e-wallets. These are transactional tools, not savings accounts. Excess balance in a gaming e-wallet can tempt over-depositing.

  • Check bonus-payment compatibility before depositing

    Some LongFu88 promotions exclude specific payment methods from eligibility. Verify that your chosen deposit method qualifies for the promotion you want to claim before funding. See promotions guide.

  • Open a support ticket before any chargeback

    Chargebacks on legitimate gambling deposits can trigger permanent account restrictions across industry shared databases. If a transaction looks incorrect, open a support ticket with evidence first — most payment discrepancies resolve within 24–48 hours through normal channels.

AML Reviews — What Happens When LongFu88 Asks for Documents

Licensed gambling operators are legally required to conduct anti-money laundering (AML) checks when deposits spike unusually, when large withdrawals are requested, or when account activity patterns trigger risk flags. Respond promptly with clear, consistent documentation. Common delay causes: mismatched names between ID and payment method, inconsistent account information, or incomplete document quality. Clear documents with matching name fields resolve AML review requests significantly faster.
