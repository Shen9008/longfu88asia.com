LongFu88 Sportsbook Review — Asian Handicap Explained

Asian handicap is the preferred betting format across Malaysia, Vietnam, Indonesia, and Thailand — and it is the core of the LongFu88 sportsbook. Unlike European 1X2 betting which includes a draw outcome, Asian handicap eliminates the draw by giving one team a goal advantage. A –0.5 handicap means the favoured team must win by at least 1 goal; a +0.5 handicap means the underdog can draw and your bet still wins. Quarter handicaps (–0.25, +0.25) split your stake — half on the full handicap, half on the half handicap — meaning a push on one half returns 50% of your stake. Understanding quarter-line splits is the single biggest edge improvement for new Asian handicap bettors on LongFu88.
